Open fencing and private lessons

Both are by appointment, in the open slots shown in the week above. Call 770-366-7633 to book.

  • Open fencing: fencing experience required. Unlimited on either plan, or a $25 strip fee.
  • Private lessons: $25 per 20 minutes. Evening lessons around class times can usually be arranged.

What happens in class

  • Footwork: advance, retreat and lunge lines, working on distance and tempo.
  • Tactical drills: two-on-one and one-step exercises, with John calling the actions.
  • Physical conditioning: short lower-body and mobility sets to protect knees and ankles.
  • Open sparring: timed bouts to 5 points, rotating partners.

Competition

We travel to local USA Fencing tournaments across Georgia and the Southeast, and John strip-coaches at every event we attend. Competing is optional.

Membership and waivers

Competitive classes require a current FIE, AAU, USFA, OGHSFL or GHSFL competitive membership. Bring the signed Emergency Contact form and Accident Waiver and Release of Liability form on your first day.
