Never fenced? Start here

Adult Beginner runs twice a week: Monday 9:00 to 10:00 PM and Friday 8:30 to 10:00 PM. Both are general beginner classes. They teach the principles of fencing to new fencers, with the basics taught using a foil, and are not tied to one weapon. You do not need to know anything about the sport before the first class.

A first-time fencer does not need to buy anything. All equipment is available at the club. If you later decide to compete in local tournaments, you will be expected to buy your own.

Your first class

  • Cost: $35 for a single class, or $110 a month on the Basic plan.
  • Bring: the signed Emergency Contact form and Accident Waiver and Release of Liability form from Forms and links.
  • Before you come: call 770-366-7633 or send a message so we can point you to the right class.

Already fence?

Come to the class for your weapon and level. If you’re not sure where you fit, call and we’ll talk it through.

Foil

  • Open / Pre-competitive Foil: Wednesday 8:30 to 10:00 PM. All ages. Basic plan.
  • Foil Competitive: Saturday 2:30 to 4:00 PM. All ages. Competitive plan.

Épée

  • Épée Beginner / Pre-competitive: Monday and Wednesday 7:00 to 8:30 PM. Ages 12 and up, adults included. Basic plan.
  • Adult Épée Competitive: Tuesday and Thursday 7:30 to 10:00 PM. Competitive plan.

Any weapon

  • Focus on Footwork: Friday 6:30 to 8:00 PM. All levels. Basic plan.
  • Open fencing: weekday afternoons by appointment. Fencing experience required. Unlimited on either plan, or a $25 strip fee.
  • Private lessons: $25 per 20 minutes, by appointment, in the open slots on the schedule.

Competitive classes

Competitive classes need the Competitive plan ($175 a month, with one lesson a week) and a current FIE, AAU, USFA, OGHSFL or GHSFL competitive membership.

Coaching and facility

Classes are taught by head coach John Terris, a Prévot of foil and épée with the United States Fencing Coaches Association and the Académie d’Armes Internationale. He has coached college teams at Emory University, Georgia Tech, the University of Georgia and Kennesaw State University, where he is an adjunct fencing instructor.

The club is at 2520 Shallowford Rd, Suite J, Marietta, in Cobb County in Atlanta’s northern suburbs. See the training room.
