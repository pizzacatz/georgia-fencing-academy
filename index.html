About Georgia Fencing Academy

Georgia Fencing Academy (GFA) is a fencing club at 2520 Shallowford Rd, Suite J, Marietta, GA 30066, in Cobb County in Atlanta’s northern suburbs. It teaches the Olympic sport of fencing in two weapons, foil and épée (also spelled epee), with classes at beginner, pre-competitive and competitive level for youth, teens and adults. Classes run Monday to Saturday, and open fencing and private lessons are by appointment on weekday afternoons.

Membership is $110 a month on the Basic plan or $175 a month on the Competitive plan, which includes one lesson a week. A single drop-in class is $35, a private lesson is $25 per 20 minutes, and the strip fee for open fencing is $25.

The head coach, owner and operator is John Terris, a Prévot of foil and épée with the United States Fencing Coaches Association and the Académie d’Armes Internationale. He has coached teams at Emory University, Georgia Tech, the University of Georgia and Kennesaw State University, where he is an adjunct fencing instructor. To ask about joining, call 770-366-7633 or email gofencegeorgia@gmail.com.

Common questions

Where is Georgia Fencing Academy?

Georgia Fencing Academy is at 2520 Shallowford Rd, Suite J, Marietta, GA 30066, in Cobb County in Atlanta’s northern suburbs. Call 770-366-7633 before your first visit.

Which fencing weapons does Georgia Fencing Academy teach?

Georgia Fencing Academy teaches foil and épée (also spelled epee), with classes at beginner, pre-competitive and competitive level in Marietta, Georgia.

How much do fencing classes cost at Georgia Fencing Academy?

A single drop-in class is $35. The Basic plan is $110 a month and the Competitive plan is $175 a month, which includes one lesson a week. Private lessons are $25 per 20 minutes, and the strip fee for open fencing is $25.

When are fencing classes held?

Georgia Fencing Academy holds classes Monday to Saturday. Weekday classes run in the evening, starting between 5:30 PM and 9:00 PM, and Saturday classes run from 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Open fencing and private lessons are by appointment on weekday afternoons from 2:00 PM. The full timetable is on the schedule page.

What ages are the classes for?

Georgia Fencing Academy has classes for youth, teens and adults. The youngest class on the schedule, Épée Youth Pre-competitive, is for ages 8 to 11. Épée Beginner / Pre-competitive is for ages 12 and up, and there are separate adult classes.

I’ve never fenced. Can I join?

Yes. At Georgia Fencing Academy in Marietta, adults start in Adult Beginner on Monday at 9:00 PM or Friday at 8:30 PM. The class teaches the principles of fencing, with the basics taught using a foil. Ages 12 and up can start in Épée Beginner / Pre-competitive on Monday or Wednesday at 7:00 PM. Call 770-366-7633 first so we can point you to the right class.

I’ve fenced before. Where do I start?

Come to a class or an open fencing night. John will watch you fence and suggest where to join: pre-competitive to shake off the rust, or competitive if you’re already bouting regularly.

Foil or épée?

Georgia Fencing Academy teaches both. Foil has right-of-way rules and a smaller target, so it rewards technique and timing. Épée has simpler rules and the whole body is target. If you’re not sure, start with a beginner class and ask the coach after you’ve tried it.

Do I need my own equipment?

No. A first-time fencer at Georgia Fencing Academy does not need to buy anything; all equipment is available at the club. Fencers who intend to compete in local tournaments are expected to buy their own equipment. The equipment links include two fencing suppliers and a sizing chart.

What do I bring the first day?

Bring the signed Emergency Contact form and the signed Accident Waiver and Release of Liability form to your first class at Georgia Fencing Academy. Both can be downloaded from Forms and links.

Can I try a single class?

Yes. A single drop-in class at Georgia Fencing Academy costs $35, with no membership plan needed. Call 770-366-7633 first so we can point you to the right class.

What’s the difference between the Basic and Competitive plans?

The Basic plan is $110 a month and covers every class except Competitive classes, plus unlimited open fencing and a monthly one-on-one progress session. The Competitive plan is $175 a month and adds the Competitive classes and one lesson per week. Competitive classes need a current FIE, AAU, USFA, OGHSFL or GHSFL competitive membership.

Who is the coach at Georgia Fencing Academy?

The head coach, owner and operator is John Terris. He is a Prévot of foil and épée with the United States Fencing Coaches Association and the Académie d’Armes Internationale, and a Level 2 épée coach certified through the USFA Coaches College. He has coached teams at Emory University, Georgia Tech, the University of Georgia and Kennesaw State University.
