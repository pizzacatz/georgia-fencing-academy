Classes by age

Ages 8 to 11

Épée Youth Pre-competitive meets three times a week: Monday and Wednesday 5:30 to 6:30 PM and Saturday 11:00 AM to 12:00 PM. It is the youngest class on the schedule and is covered by the Basic plan. If your child has never fenced, call 770-366-7633 first and we’ll tell you which class fits.

Ages 12 and up

Épée Beginner / Pre-competitive meets Monday and Wednesday 7:00 to 8:30 PM. The two foil classes, Open / Pre-competitive Foil on Wednesday 8:30 to 10:00 PM and Foil Competitive on Saturday 2:30 to 4:00 PM, have no age range. Focus on Footwork on Friday 6:30 to 8:00 PM is open to all levels.

Competitive youth and high school

High School and Youth Épée Competitive meets Tuesday and Thursday 6:00 to 7:30 PM, and Youth Épée Competitive meets Saturday 12:00 to 1:30 PM. Competitive classes need the Competitive plan, which includes one lesson a week, and a current FIE, AAU, USFA, OGHSFL or GHSFL competitive membership. High school league fencers also bring the signed OGHSFL Participation Waiver (PDF).

Head coach John Terris has coached the high school teams at North Gwinnett, Lanier and Lassiter, and college teams at Emory, Georgia Tech, the University of Georgia and Kennesaw State.

For parents

Cost

  • Single class: $35, a good way to find out whether your child likes it.
  • Basic plan: $110 a month. Any class except Competitive classes, unlimited open fencing, and a monthly one-on-one progress session.
  • Competitive plan: $175 a month. Every class, one lesson a week, unlimited open fencing.
  • Private lessons: $25 per 20 minutes, by appointment.

Equipment and forms

  • Nothing to buy to start. All equipment is available at the club. Fencers who go on to compete in local tournaments are expected to buy their own; the equipment links include a sizing chart.
  • First day: bring the signed Emergency Contact form and Accident Waiver and Release of Liability form from Forms and links.
  • Questions: call 770-366-7633 or send a message.
